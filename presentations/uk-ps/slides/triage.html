Act

The first hour of an investigation,
done before you arrive.

01

Detect

The code appears and a rule fires within the minute. One alert per object, so nothing is buried.

02

Check

Is this object already being investigated? One incident stays one case, however many alerts arrive.

03

Gather

Recent movements, ownership, an independent position check, and any news reporting the same event.

04

Assess

An AI analyst weighs that evidence and says whether this looks genuine, with its reasoning attached.

05

Hand over

A case is opened with everything in it. Credible threats go to the duty channel; likely false alarms are marked.

under 2 minutes

from transmission to a briefed case. No one had to be awake for it.
