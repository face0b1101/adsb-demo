Picture to action

Five steps between a transmission
and someone doing something.

01

Live positions arrive

A continuous public feed, taken in as it is broadcast.

02

Every point gets a place

Country, region and nearest known site, added on the way in.

03

Common operating picture

One map and one timeline the whole room can look at.

04

Ask, or be briefed

Plain-English questions, plus a summary waiting each morning.

05

Signal to first triage

An emergency code opens a case with the background gathered.

Four jobs, in order: see, place, explore, act. The rest of this briefing walks the same line.
