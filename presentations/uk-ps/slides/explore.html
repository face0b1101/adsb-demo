Asked of the live data

What is over Scotland right now, and is anything behaving oddly?

Answer

Forty-one aircraft are currently within Scottish airspace. Most are scheduled traffic on the Glasgow and Edinburgh approaches. Three things are worth a look:

  • One aircraft holding north-west of Glasgow for 26 minutes, well outside a normal pattern.
  • Two low, slow tracks over the Highlands with no filed operator.
  • No emergency codes currently being transmitted in this area.
